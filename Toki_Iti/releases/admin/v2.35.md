## Highlights

### Workspaces

Introducing Workspaces — a brand-new way for agents to organize, manage, and collaborate on tickets. Instead of relying on assigned or status-based filter lists, agents can now build their own personalized ticket workflow: create a workspace, join a teammate's workspace, and arrange tickets exactly the way that makes sense to them.

#### Why We Built This

Status fields are great for tracking where a ticket is in its lifecycle, but they were never meant to answer "what should I work on next, and in what order?" Workspaces closes that gap by decoupling ticket organization from ticket status. Agents can create **groups** within a workspace, and freely **customize and reorder** them to reflect their own process. Tickets can be added to a workspace from multiple entry points and methods, making it easy to pull relevant work into your queue no matter where you find it.

#### Real-Time Collaboration

Workspaces aren't just personal — they're built for teamwork. Agents can join each other's workspaces to triage together, share visibility into priorities, and hand off tickets seamlessly by moving them between shared groups. It's a new way to collaborate that goes beyond simply assigning and reassigning tickets.

#### Automation with Webhooks

To keep workspaces effortless to maintain, we've added webhook support for adding, moving, and removing tickets programmatically. This means existing bots, scripts, and integrations can plug directly into your workspace, keeping it current automatically as work evolves.

#### The Everyday Impact

- **No more digging through filtered lists** — bring the tickets that matter into your workspace and order them your way.
- **Reorder on the fly** as priorities shift, without touching ticket status.
- **Collaborate directly** by joining shared workspaces and moving tickets between groups.
- **Automate upkeep** with webhooks so your workspace stays fresh without manual effort.

Workspaces give agents true ownership over how they manage their work — a flexible, personalized alternative to static ticket lists.
